<h2>Server Rules:</h2>
Check our server rules by clicking on the <b>Rules</b> Tab in the top right, it will be assumed that you have already read them in the case of an incident. If you aren't aware of our rules, you could be warned by an admin or jobbanned! Game & Job Bans can be appealed on our <a href="https://discord.gg/0oRsdvoS1DDd5Rv0">Discord</a> in the <i>#ban-appeals</i> channel. <br>
Poor conduct as Command Staff, Security or Silicon roles will not be tolerated, and will be treated more harshly should rules be broken.
Do not discuss on-going events in OOC chat, or you will be muted.<br>
If you are not an antagonist, do not kill unless in self defense within sufficent escalation.
Murderboning is not allowed, even as an antagonist role. Sitting around and watching isn't fun!<br>
If you have a problem or have a question, <b>adminhelp it!</b> You can do this by pressing <b>F1.</b>
<i>We exist for a reason, and would like to keep the server fun. We can't predict everything, and questions are likely to crop up during play, especially with regard to antagonists. Asking won't hurt anyone.</i>
<center><b>Golden Rule:</b> <i>Do unto others as you would have done unto you, try to keep things enjoyable for everyone!</i></center>
<h3>New Player Information:</h3>
Ensure you enable your neural lace in <b>Character Setup</b> under <b>Body</b> to be cloned, or your body will be unrecoverable without cyborgification or a full-body prosthesis! <br>
You are able to respawn after a 10 minute timer has passed on death. You can respawn by going to the <b>OOC</b> Tab and clicking the <b>Respawn</b> Verb.
Ensure you choose a new character on respawn and don't use your previous character's knowledge for your new character during the round!
